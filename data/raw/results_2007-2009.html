DSP Meeting Results, 2007-2009

Below are the results of the 2007-2009 MSBA's Designer Selection Panel meetings. The results are displayed in descending order, starting with the results of the most recent DSP meeting.

Meeting Date: Tuesday, December 29, 2009

Arlington – Thompson Elementary School

Application review at the December 15, 2009 DSP meeting and interviews at the December 29th 2009 meeting resulted in the following ranking of applicants:

  1. HMFH Architects, Inc.
  2. Turowski2 Architecture, Inc.
  3. Flansburgh Associates, Inc. and Jonathan Levi Architects LLC (tied)

There were no informational interviews at this DSP meeting.
